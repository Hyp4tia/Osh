## 🎯 Why Choose Osh?

- **Finder-First Workflow**: Preview documents instantly without opening a heavy editor window.
- **AI Agent Skill Native**: Open, inspect, and edit packaged `.skill` archives (`SKILL.md`) with byte-for-byte asset preservation.
- **Scientific Visualization**: Rich offline rendering for KaTeX, Typst, Mermaid diagrams, Vega-Lite charts, and Graphviz graphs.
- **Uncompromised Typography & RTL**: Built-in support for Right-to-Left writing systems (Arabic and Hebrew) with proper bidirectional layout mirroring.
- **Transparent Security**: Public security audit report, App Sandbox enforcement, strict DOMPurify sanitization, and Content Security Policies.
